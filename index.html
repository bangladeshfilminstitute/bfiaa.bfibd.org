About Us

One Institute.
Thousands of Stories.

The Bangladesh Film Institute Alumni Association (BFIAA) is the official alumni organisation of the Bangladesh Film Institute (BFI), founded in 2005 by the acclaimed filmmaker and author Tanvir Mokammel.

BFIAA was established to bring together the growing family of BFI graduates — filmmakers, cinematographers, editors, producers, and film enthusiasts — who have been shaped by Bangladesh's most dedicated institution for cinema education.

Over more than two decades, BFI has produced over 1,000 graduates across 86+ batches, offering programmes ranging from the Film Appreciation Course to the Online Filmmaking Course. The Alumni Association serves as the living thread that keeps this community connected, active, and creative.

Through its biennial conferences, Uralchitra magazine, Cine Club Adda film screenings, and cultural programmes, BFIAA continues to champion the cause of independent cinema and film culture in Bangladesh.

Founder & Patron

Tanvir Mokammel

"A renowned Bangladeshi filmmaker, author, and the guiding force behind the Bangladesh Film Institute."

Tanvir Mokammel has profoundly shaped the landscape of independent cinema in Bangladesh. He has directed eight fiction films and seventeen documentaries, earning ten National Film Awards for his exceptional work.

Beyond filmmaking, he is a prolific writer with twenty-eight books published, including novels, articles, poems, and short stories that reflect deeply on Bangladeshi history and culture.

For his lifelong dedication to the arts, he was honored with the Ekushey Padak, the second-highest civilian award in Bangladesh. Currently, he serves as the director of BFI, continuing to mentor the next generation of filmmakers.

Our Mission

What We Stand For

🎬

Strengthen Film Culture

Promote and nurture a vibrant film culture across Bangladesh by bringing together alumni who are active voices in independent and documentary filmmaking.

🤝

Build the Alumni Network

Maintain a register of all BFI graduates and create meaningful connections between alumni working in film, media, education, and related creative fields.

📚

Support Cinema Education

Assist and cooperate with the Bangladesh Film Institute in its educational programmes, workshops, masterclasses, and the overall mission of cinema literacy.

📖

Publish & Archive

Publish Uralchitra, the association's magazine on film culture, and maintain an archive of alumni work, screening records, and institutional memory.

🎥

Screen & Celebrate

Organise Cine Club Adda screenings, film appreciation events, and celebrate the work of alumni filmmakers through festivals and retrospectives.

🌐

Global Reach

Connect Bangladesh's film community with international film institutes, festivals, and networks — giving BFI alumni a global platform.

Our Journey

A Decade of Togetherness

▶ 2005
Bangladesh Film Institute Founded
BFI established by documentary filmmaker Tanvir Mokammel, offering the first Film Appreciation Course to a pioneering batch of students in Dhaka.
▶ 2009
BFIAA Formally Established
The Bangladesh Film Institute Alumni Association is formed by graduates of the early batches, creating an organised body to keep the BFI community connected.
▶ EARLY 2010s
First Uralchitra Publication
Launch of Uralchitra, BFIAA's film culture magazine, providing alumni a platform for film writing, criticism, and creative expression.
▶ 2019
Cine Club Adda Begins
Launch of the Cine Club Adda screening series — a regular film gathering for alumni and film enthusiasts to discuss world cinema.
▶ DECEMBER 17, 2021
BFIAA Biennial Conference 2021
A landmark event bringing together hundreds of alumni. The conference featured the release of Uralchitra, panel discussions, film screenings, and the election of the new executive committee.
▶ 2022–2023
New Executive Committee Elected
A new executive committee is elected and registered, with Tanvir Mokammel continuing as patron and advisor of the association.
▶ 2023
Uralchitra 2023 & 30-Year Celebration
The association marks BFI's journey with the 2023 edition of Uralchitra magazine and special cultural events commemorating 30 years of Tanvir Mokammel's filmmaking legacy.
▶ PRESENT
1000+ Strong & Growing
BFIAA today represents a community of over 1,000 alumni across 86+ batches, united by their love for cinema and the shared experience of BFI's unique film education.
Publication

উড়ালচিত্র
Uralchitra

Uralchitra (literally, "the flying image") is the official magazine of the Bangladesh Film Institute Alumni Association. There are a total of 4 publications of Uralchitra released so far.

The latest publication is a Special Edition on Tanvir Mokammel, whose theme was "30 Years in Documentary Films of Tanvir Mokammel". This February 2023 issue celebrates his legacy and documentary practice in Bangladesh.

Each edition brings together essays, reviews, interviews, and creative writing from alumni and film scholars. It serves as a document of the BFI community's engagement with world cinema and as an archive of Bangladeshi film culture.
