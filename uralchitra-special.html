30 Years in Documentary Films of Tanvir Mokammel

Uralchitra (literally, "the flying image") is the official publication of the Bangladesh Film Institute Alumni Association. The fourth and latest volume is a highly anticipated Special Edition celebrating the legacy of eminent filmmaker Tanvir Mokammel.

To mark a monumental milestone—30 Years of Documentary Filmmaking by Tanvir Mokammel—the BFIAA organized a grand three-day retrospective festival from March 6th to 8th, 2023. Held at the prestigious Jatiya Chitrashala Auditorium (National Art Gallery) at the Bangladesh Shilpakala Academy in Dhaka, the open-to-all event showcased seven of his most acclaimed documentary films.

The festival was a vibrant gathering for cinema lovers and scholars alike. It featured an exclusive masterclass on the "Art of Documentary", engaging "Meet the Director" sessions, and a heartwarming celebration of the director's birthday. The culminating highlight of the event was the official unveiling of this Special Edition of Uralchitra, which serves as a permanent archive of essays, reviews, and profound reflections on his three-decade journey in independent cinema.
