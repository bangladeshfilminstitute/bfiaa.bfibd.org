ত্রিশ বছরের অবিচল সৃজনযাত্রা

মার্চ ২০২৩-এ বাংলাদেশ ফিল্ম ইনস্টিটিউট অ্যালামনাই এসোসিয়েশন (BFIAA) আয়োজন করে তিন দিনব্যাপী এক ঐতিহাসিক চলচ্চিত্র উৎসব। বরেণ্য চলচ্চিত্রকার তানভীর মোকাম্মেলের প্রামাণ্যচিত্র নির্মাণের তিন দশক উদযাপনে জাতীয় চিত্রশালা মিলনায়তনে সমবেত হন শত শত চলচ্চিত্রপ্রেমী ও গবেষক।

Archival Chronicle & Legacy

The three-day retrospective presented a comprehensive chronological journey through Tanvir Mokammel's monumental non-fiction filmography. As one of South Asia's most distinguished documentary practitioners, Tanvir Mokammel has unflinchingly documented liberation struggles, religious pluralism, marginalized cultures, environmental crises, and subcontinental history throughout his three-decade career.

From the historic struggles of working-class garment workers to the oral history of 1971 survivors, the displacement of indigenous peoples in the Chittagong Hill Tracts, and the lingering scars of the 1947 Bengal Partition, the festival served as an enduring archive of human resilience and moral witness.

Festival Filmography · প্রদর্শিত প্রামাণ্যচিত্রসমূহ

The 7 Screened Documentaries

01
The Garment Girls of Bangladesh (২০০১)

Exploration of working-class women laboring in Dhaka's textile and readymade garment sector, chronicling their socio-economic resilience.

02
A Tale of the Jamuna River (২০০২)

Life, riverbank erosion, displacement, and environmental resilience along the majestic Jamuna river basin.

03
Tajuddin Ahmad: An Unsung Hero (২০০৭)

Landmark biographical and political documentary on Bangladesh's first Prime Minister and wartime architect of the 1971 liberation.

04
1971 (২০০৬)

Monumental multi-hour epic oral history capturing the harrowing testimony and grassroots heroism of the Bangladesh Liberation War.

05
The Promised Land (২০০৭)

Poignant chronicle of the Urdu-speaking communities (Biharis) stranded in refugee settlements across Bangladesh.

06
Teardrops of Karnaphuli (২০১৪)

Devastation and forced displacement of indigenous Chakma and hill peoples caused by the Kaptai Dam in the Chittagong Hill Tracts.

07
Seemantorekha (The Borderline) (২০১৭)

An epic, meditative reflection on the 1947 Bengal Partition, crossing borders to investigate its enduring multi-generational trauma.

Academic Masterclass · চলচ্চিত্র পাঠ

The Art & Ethics of Documentary Filmmaking

Alongside the film screenings, Tanvir Mokammel conducted an exclusive Masterclass for BFI alumni and young cinematographers. The session dissected non-fiction aesthetics, ethical responsibilities when filming vulnerable communities, research methodologies, and cinematic storytelling.

Archival Publication · বিশেষ প্রকাশনা

Uralchitra Special Retrospective Edition

The festival culminated in the historic unveiling of the Uralchitra Special Retrospective Edition — a 200+ page commemorative volume containing critical essays, filmography analyses, archival photographs, and scholarly reviews of Tanvir Mokammel's cinematic oeuvre.
