Ahmed

সৈয়দ ওয়াসিউদ্দিন আহমেদ
Documentary Filmmaker Screenwriter Producer Assistant Director

A passionate documentary filmmaker and screenwriter from Khulna, Bangladesh. A graduate of the Bangladesh Film Institute (2009), Syed Oasiuddin Ahmed has dedicated over a decade to crafting intimate, socially conscious films that illuminate the lives and stories of ordinary people. His works span documentaries and short fiction films, marked by a humane gaze and quiet visual poetry.
